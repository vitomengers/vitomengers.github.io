As part of the event series "Wissen zwischen Büchern" in Berlin's public libraries, author Emma Braslavsky reads from her novel "Ich bin dein Mensch", in which a renowned couples therapist who believes love is only possible between real humans orders an android partner tailored perfectly to her. After the reading, Anna Lange and I from the Science of Intelligence Cluster join her and the audience in a discussion: How much science is in science fiction? How close is the novel to what is technically possible today? And what separates genuine understanding from perfect simulation?
